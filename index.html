    THE IDEA

    AI needs an instrument of measurement.

    Modern AI systems are increasingly powerful, but their reliability is still difficult to measure across data, models, environments and time.

    SCALE-X aims to build that measurement layer: a scientific, explainable framework for understanding what an AI system knows, where it fails and how its reliability changes.
